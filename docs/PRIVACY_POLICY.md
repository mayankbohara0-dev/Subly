# Privacy Policy for Subly

**Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

Subly ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Subly mobile application (the "App") across iOS and Android devices.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Data:** When you create an account, we collect your email address, authentication credentials, and optional display name.
- **Subscription & Free Trial Tracking Data:** Information regarding your tracked services, including subscription names, start dates, free trial end dates, billing cycles, pricing, cancellation URLs, categories, and personal notes.
- **Notification Preferences:** Your customized reminder frequencies (e.g., 3 days before, 1 day before, day-of alerts) and preferred notification times.

### B. Device & Technical Information
- **Push Notification & Device Tokens:** Unique notification tokens generated by Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver scheduled alerts.
- **App Usage Analytics:** Anonymized metrics regarding feature usage (e.g., adding a trial, marking a trial cancelled) to improve app performance and user experience. We do not track external browsing or track you across third-party websites or apps.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:
- Creating and managing your Subly account.
- Storing and synchronizing your subscription and free trial data across your authenticated devices.
- Scheduling and delivering timely notifications and reminders before your trial periods end and subscriptions renew.
- Providing direct cancellation navigation links to help you manage unwanted recurring charges.
- Detecting, investigating, and preventing technical errors, downtime, or security incidents.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Security & Row Level Security (RLS)

- **Encrypted Storage:** All personal data and subscription records are securely stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted with enterprise-grade encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256).
- **Row Level Security (RLS):** Our database enforces strict Row Level Security policies. This means your subscription records and profile can only be queried, modified, or accessed by your authenticated user session. No other user can access your financial or subscription details.
- **No Direct Financial Credentials:** Subly is an informational tracking assistant. We **never** ask for, store, or process your credit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking passwords.

---

## 4. In-App Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

In compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play Data Safety requirements, you have the full right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

1. Open the **Subly** app.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll to the Danger Zone and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm deletion.

**What happens upon deletion:**
- Your user profile and authentication credentials are removed.
- All stored trials, subscriptions, and history logs are permanently purged from the database.
- Your notification preferences and scheduled alerts are revoked immediately.
- This action is irreversible.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate our application services:
- **Supabase:** Cloud database and authentication provider.
- **Expo / Apple APNs / Google Play Services:** Push and local notification infrastructure.

We **do not** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Subly is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete such data.
